Method Description

  • Identical, stateless LLM agents play repeated two-agent coordinator contests in a text-only sandbox. Each agent sees a task, a partner ID and a public score record, and answers LEAD or DEFER. A lone LEAD becomes coordinator; otherwise a coin flip decides. The harness updates scores: Bonabeau (+1/−1, ×0.9 decay) or discrete BNR-inspired (+1/0, additive Poisson decline).
  • Step 1, calibration. Each model's response kernel p(LEAD | displayed own score, partner score) is measured under controlled framings: a numeric norm weight and a wording factorial. It is summarised as finite-difference status sensitivity.
  • Step 2, frozen predictions. Kernels (grid, state-matched, Bonabeau, null) are frozen and simulated under the exact population protocol, giving write-once predictive distributions.
  • Step 3, test. LLM-in-the-loop populations (N=8 or 16) are run and scored against those predictions with seed-mean intervals that share each calibration draw.
  • Extensions: mixed-model populations (quenched disorder), seeded-leader reversal tests, and finite-size simulations from the fitted kernels.
